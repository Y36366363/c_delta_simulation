\section{Two scientific components}

Let $T_X$ and $T_Y$ be Huber location functionals, with MAD scales used in
their estimating equations, and define the observation-level radii
\begin{equation}
R_X=|X-T_X|, \qquad R_Y=|Y-T_Y|.
\end{equation}
The profile component is
\begin{equation}
\rhoP=\operatorname{Cor}(R_X,R_Y).
\end{equation}
It asks whether the same labelled observation is salient in both variables.
For nondegenerate profiles, $\cds$ is related to this correlation by
\begin{equation}
\cds=1+\rhoP\operatorname{CV}(R_X)\operatorname{CV}(R_Y).
\end{equation}

With an independent copy $(X',Y')$, the population Mantel component is
\begin{equation}
\rhoM=\operatorname{Cor}(|X-X'|,|Y-Y'|).
\end{equation}
It asks whether the complete dyadic distance geometry agrees. The two local
weak nulls are
\begin{equation}
H_{0P}:\rhoP=0, \qquad H_{0M}:\rhoM=0.
\end{equation}
They are scientifically related but are not the same null hypothesis.

\section{Fully recomputed studentized permutation}

For component $j\in\{P,M\}$ and permutation $\pi$, define
\begin{equation}
Z_j^\pi=\frac{\widehat\rho_j^\pi}{\widehat{\operatorname{SE}}_j^\pi},
\qquad
p_j=\frac{1+\sum_{r=1}^{R}
I\{|Z_{j,r}^\pi|\geq |Z_{j,\mathrm{obs}}|\}}{R+1}.
\end{equation}
For the profile statistic, every permutation recomputes the five joint
profile moments, the correlation gradient, the two location-nuisance
coefficients, the paired ordering of the location influence values, and the
complete influence variance. For Mantel, every permutation recomputes the
five distance-kernel moments, all node-conditional kernel means, the
order-two Hajek projection, and its node-level variance. Marginal Huber/MAD
fits are not refitted because they are invariant to permutations of the
pairing.

The two local p-values are adjusted by Holm. With two hypotheses, the smaller
raw p-value is multiplied by two and the larger adjusted p-value is the
maximum of the first adjusted value and the larger raw value. Holm does not
require subset pivotality, but it does require valid local p-values.

\section{Main iid calibration}

At $n=80$, 600 datasets per cell and 499 permutations gave the following
results.

\begin{table}[ht]

\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\hline
Scenario & Profile reject & Mantel reject & Holm true-null error\\
\hline
Signed-lognormal global null & .0650 & .0550 & .0500\\
Profile null, Mantel alternative & .0483 & .9317 & .0417\\
Calibrated-mixture global null & .0367 & .0383 & .0300\\
Mantel null, profile alternative & .6433 & .0467 & .0367\\
Both positive alternatives & .9833 & .9983 & ---\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

At $n=160$, the tested global- and partial-null Holm errors ranged from
.0225 to .0350. One individual Mantel local-null row reached .0700, while its
Holm family error was .0350. Thus the simulations support pointwise candidacy,
not uniform finite-sample validity.

\section{Heavy-tail, skewness, and boundary stress test}

A focused pilot used $n=80$, 300 datasets per row, and 199 permutations.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{Stress-test rejection rates.}
\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\hline
Scenario & Profile local & Mantel local & Holm true-null error\\
\hline
Independent $t_5$ & .0333 & .0367 & .0200\\
Independent $t_3$ & .0333 & .0567 & .0233\\
Independent strongly skew margins & .0400 & .0667 & .0300\\
Profile null, heavy-tail sign link & .0533 & .9267 & .0533\\
Profile null, nearly constant radii & .9533 & 1.0000 & .9533\\
Mantel null, profile alternative & .6500 & .0333 & .0300\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The first, second, and third rows are global nulls. The fourth row retains a
true profile null while creating strong dyadic sign agreement. The sixth row
retains a true Mantel null while making the profile component nonzero. The
regular heavy-tail, skew, and partial-null rows are broadly compatible with
nominal behavior after Holm adjustment.

The fifth row deliberately approaches a nonregular boundary. Both margins
are separated symmetric sign mixtures and the independent population radii
have standard deviation only .03. At the population reference, $\rhoP=0$.
In samples, however, the low-density median/MAD region is unstable and the
$O_p(n^{-1/2})$ fitted-location error can dominate the genuine radius
variation. Shared signs then make the two fitted errors move together. The
mean estimated profile correlation was .9123, and studentization could not
repair the failed regular expansion.

This result motivates explicit regularity conditions: a locally unique
Huber/MAD solution, adequate density at the relevant median and MAD
quantiles, and both profile variances bounded away from zero. If these checks
fail, the profile component should be reported as weakly identified or
undetermined rather than assigned an ordinary local p-value.

\section{Why the six-building case remains unresolved}

The building simulation used six independent buildings with 20 rooms each.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{Familywise error under building dependence.}
\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\hline
Null model & Room-iid & Building-summed $t_5$ & Linear sign-flip\\
\hline
Gaussian buildings & .2450 & .0405 & .1800\\
Skew-scale buildings & .3685 & .0735 & .2360\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Room-iid inference is therefore rejected. The building-summed $t_5$ method
was adequate in the Gaussian row but not under skewed building scales, and
the linearized sign-flip was invalid in both rows. Increasing the number of
buildings to 12 or 24 did not repair the skew-scale row, suggesting that the
problem is not only degrees of freedom. Global Mantel geometry contains
cross-building dyads, so a room-level influence projected and then summed by
building may omit relevant finite-cluster U-statistic structure.

\section{Decision requested and proposed next step}

Before deriving another building correction, the target should be fixed:

\begin{description}
\item[Average within-building target.] Calculate one profile/Mantel effect
vector per building and use a small-sample multivariate procedure. This is
comparatively interpretable, but cross-building dyads are excluded by
definition.
\item[One global building target.] Preserve all within- and cross-building
dyads. This is closer to the current pooled geometry, but requires a new
cluster-level projection for the distance U-functional before resampling.
\end{description}

The immediate recommendation is to retain studentized permutation plus Holm
as an iid research candidate, add a nondegeneracy/stability screen, and defer
formal six-building component discovery until the scientific target above is
chosen.
